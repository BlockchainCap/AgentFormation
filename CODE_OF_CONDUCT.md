# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and patient. Harassment, threats, discrimination,
doxxing, and publication of another person's private data are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through
the repository owner rather than escalating them in a public issue. Enforcement is
handled by the maintainers using their best judgment and without a guaranteed
response time.
